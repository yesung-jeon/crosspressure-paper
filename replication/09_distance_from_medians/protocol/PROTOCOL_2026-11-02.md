# Distance from the medians: protocol (written before any result, 2026-11-02)

Question: is the Section 5 placement gap produced by member-Congresses near the medians?

Concern: cross-pressure is a two-median classification, so members barely across a median could produce the low recall of
cross-pressured member-Congresses.

Rows: the Section 5 rows (Democratic and Republican member-Congresses from 1973 with both the economic and the abortion scale; map and
vote quadrants exactly as `05_directions_and_map/code/06_vote_vs_map.py` with `00_shared/splits.py` split_hi). Gate: pooled recall
for aligned and cross-pressured rows equals `08_supplementary_checks/benchmark/benchmark_recall.json` N2_recognized/<m>/all_rows
(|diff| < 1e-12).

Distance from the medians: within each Congress and chamber, each roll-call scale is centred on its median and divided by its standard
deviation; m = min(|z_economic|, |z_abortion|), the distance to the nearer of the two cut lines. Rows are split into terciles of m
(cut points over all rows, common to both models).
- Primary: recall of aligned and of cross-pressured rows, and their difference, in the top tercile (furthest from both medians).
- Secondary: the same in each tercile, and among rows with m >= 0.5.
Intervals: members resampled (2,000 draws, seed from "V2"). Reading: if the aligned minus cross-pressured gap in the top tercile has an
interval excluding zero and is not smaller than half of the full-sample gap, the gap is not produced by near-median rows; if it falls
below half of the full-sample gap, near-median rows account for much of it. Both outcomes are reported.

Outputs: `out/v2.json`; pipeline step `validity.v2`. Every input is an output of an earlier step; nothing is refitted.
